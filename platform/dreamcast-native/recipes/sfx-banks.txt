# The sound effect banks of build_sfx.sh: which rendered flows are resident together, and their sample coding.
#   BANK CODING KEY...   KEY: a key of render.tsv (tools/prepare_sfx.cpp), or a hex range FIRST-LAST of keys with
#                        the same prefix (9e01-9e2d). CODING: pcm8 (afx_vgm's default) or adpcm (afx_vgm --adpcm).
# Every bank BANK becomes sfx/BANK.afb + sfx/BANK.dsx; code/audio/sfx.c loads sfx/race.* at start-up, before any music.
#
# race: effects available during the race, resident next to its music. E0-E9 remain here for race telemetry.
#   9e01-9e2d  channel 14's effect kit: crashes, wall rub, passes, skid and rev, air drill, tool drop, coin, signal
#   ae2109-c   the start voices THREE, TWO, ONE, GO
#   ae2010     the crowd at every lap (shares 9e11's sample); ae2002/ae2003 the slot machine
#
# select: course/transmission selection and the "start your engines" sequence.
#   E0-E9 are shared with race; phase 16 starts 9e11 (crowd cheer) and AE2001 together.
#   9e11 is intentionally duplicated here because it also belongs to the race effect kit.
#
# voices: announcer phrases used beyond the countdown, including the race calls and the course/transmission selection
# prompts from include/select.h: 58 ROM-rendered ADPCM phrases. They do
# not fit beside the PCM8 race music: build_sfx.sh --voices stream (default) writes sfx/voices.pak for the streamed
# slot (NATIVE_SFX_VOICE_STREAM=1); --voices resident adds them to the race bank (beside the race music in ADPCM).
race pcm8 e0-e9
race adpcm 9e01-9e2d
race adpcm ae2109-ae210c ae2010
select pcm8 e0-e9
select adpcm 9e11 ae2001 ae2158
voices adpcm ae2002 ae2003 ae2100 ae2101 ae2105-ae2108 ae210f ae2111 ae2114-ae2117 ae211a ae2120-ae2131 ae2133-ae2138 ae215a ae215b ae215c-ae2160 ae2163-ae216d ae2170 ae2171 ae221c
